Definitions

  • bTTK: bodyshot-only time to kill.
  • hTTK: headshot-only time to kill.
  • eTTK: expected TTK assuming every shot hits.
  • vTTK: expected TTK accounting for misses and the selected accuracy.
  • TTK uses 1.25 rounds per second and excludes reload time.
