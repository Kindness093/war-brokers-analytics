# Wiki range-chart source images

These unmodified charts were downloaded from the War Brokers Wiki on October 29, 2026 for visual transcription. Each image's wiki page and original image URL, together with the transcribed values, are recorded in ../../data/wiki-range-chart-sources.json.

Attribution: War Brokers Wiki contributors, via Fandom. Wiki community content is offered under CC BY-SA unless otherwise noted: https://war-brokers.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons and https://www.fandom.com/licensing . Original image URLs provide image-specific history and attribution context. Chart images are retained for research and are not included in the deployed site's asset package.

Explosive charts describe blast distance from the explosion. The Grenade Launcher chart says 125 damage whereas its infobox says 130; the site records both separately. Shotgun chart maxima describe all nine pellets; lower bounds describe one pellet. The Sniper Rifle theoretical plateau lies beyond its marked projectile limit, so predictions past that limit are disabled.
